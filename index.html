A single-player, offline street brawler set in Barranco, Peru.

Contact support

Email renzedu@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, game version, and what happened. Please do not send passwords or other sensitive information.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No. The game has no sign-in and runs offline after installation. Its images, music, and voices are bundled with the app.

How do I start?

Rotate your iPhone to landscape. Choose Start, difficulty, fighter, and Level 1. Tap to advance the intro or use Skip.

How do I move and fight?

Touch and drag on the movement side. Use the arcade buttons to punch, kick, jump, block, and charge. Pause to see the controls diagram and change handedness.

How do I use weapons?

Stand over a weapon and punch to pick it up. Kick to throw it. Stun an enemy and move close to grab them, then use an attack to throw or strike.

How do I adjust sound?

Pause the game to change music, sound-effect, and voice volumes. Check your device volume if sound is not audible.

Is Level 2 available?

Level 1 is playable. Completing it unlocks the road to Arequipa, but Level 2 gameplay is not included in version 1.0.

The game stops responding. What should I do?

Close and reopen the app, and check for updates. If the problem continues, contact support with steps to reproduce it. Removing the app can remove locally saved progress.
