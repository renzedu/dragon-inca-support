DRAGON INCA Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026. Developer: Renzo Carpio.

No data collected by the game

DRAGON INCA is an offline, single-player game. The game does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, chat, or in-app purchases. Images, music, and voices are included with the app.

Local progress and settings

Progress, scores, and preferences are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to the developer. Removing the app may remove this local data.

Support messages

If you voluntarily email support, the developer receives your email address and the information you include solely to respond to your request. Please do not send sensitive information. You may request deletion of support correspondence by contacting the address below.

Apple and this website

Apple may process App Store, TestFlight, purchase/download, or diagnostic information under its own policies and your device settings. This support website is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical request information under its privacy policy. Neither service is an analytics or tracking service embedded in the game.
